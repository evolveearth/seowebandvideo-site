Privacy

How SEO Web and Video handles your information

Plain language, no tricks. This page lists what this site actually collects and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

This site is run by SEO Web and Video, an Evolve Earth brand (Joseph, Hawaii). Questions about your information go to info@seowebandvideo.com. We are a small business and we read every message.

What we collect when you write to us

When you use the contact form or email us, we get what you choose to send: usually your name, email, phone number if you add it, and your message. The form sends it to our inbox. We use it to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

What happens automatically when you visit

This site runs through Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks and makes it load fast. To do that, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and keeps short-lived security logs. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

Google Analytics

To understand which parts of this site people use, we use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Cloudflare Zaraz. We set it up to be as light as possible: your IP address is not sent to Google, Google signals (cross-device and ads data) is turned off, and we do not use the data for advertising. Google may store an analytics identifier in your browser. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months. You can block it with any standard content blocker or your browser's privacy settings, and the site still works.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not run advertising cookies on this site. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Who else touches your data

Only the services that run the site: Cloudflare (hosting, security, email forwarding, Zaraz), Google (Analytics), and Google (Gmail, where our inbox lives). Each handles data under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and account details as long as we need them to work with you, then delete what we no longer need. Ask us and we will delete your information sooner, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example insurance or tax records).

Your choices and rights

Email info@seowebandvideo.com to ask what we have about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop us using it. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we honor them for everyone.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we update this page and the date below. Effective October 28, 2026.
